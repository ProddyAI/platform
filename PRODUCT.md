# PRODUCT.md

*Read this before any design work. It's the brief, captured once. When a decision isn't
covered here, make it in the spirit of what is.*

---

## Proddy in one line

An agentic work-management suite. Messaging, tasks, boards, canvas, notes, calendar,
meetings, and reports in **one** workspace — so work moves from discussion to execution
without switching tools.

## Users

Small, fast teams — startups, product squads, remote teams. They live in the app all day,
carry a lot of context in their heads, and switch between *talking* and *doing* constantly.
They are competent operators, not visitors. They value their time and feel every point of
friction.

Primary jobs:

- Turn a conversation into a task, a task into a plan, a plan into shipped work — without
  re-typing it into another tool.
- Keep the team's context in one place: what was said, what's decided, what's next.
- Get AI help where it saves real time — summaries, scheduling, drafting, insights — never
  as a gimmick.

## What it replaces

Slack, Notion, Confluence, Todoist, Miro. Proddy earns its place by being one coherent
surface instead of five disconnected ones. It should *feel* like a single tool, not a set
of apps bolted together.

## Register

Product, not marketing. Design serves the task, not the pitch. Every pixel is used by
someone mid-work — density, speed, and clarity beat delight. The interface gets out of the
way.

## Brand voice

Confident, plain-spoken, warm but not chatty. We talk to operators who know what they want.

- Say what a thing does, then stop.
- Verbs over adjectives. "Summarize this thread," not "Supercharge your communication."
- Respect the reader's time and intelligence. Exclamation marks are rare.
- Calm under load. The tone doesn't change when things get busy or break.

## Design principles

- **Content first.** The user's work *is* the interface. Chrome recedes.
- **One system.** Every module speaks one grammar — shared spacing, type, color, motion. A
  button is a button everywhere.
- **Fast is a feature.** Instant feedback, real-time by default. No spinner where an
  optimistic update will do.
- **Legible density.** Pack information without clutter. Whitespace with intent, not padding
  for its own sake.
- **Motion with meaning.** Animate to explain a state change, never to decorate. Short,
  quiet, interruptible, reduced-motion aware.
- **Accessible by default.** Contrast, focus states, keyboard paths — non-negotiable, not a
  phase two.

## Anti-references (what we are not)

- Hype copy: "Boost your productivity," "Work smarter, not harder," buzzword badge-pills.
- Unmotivated gradients, glassmorphism, and floating blur-blobs behind every section.
- Stock 3D hero art and confetti/emoji celebrations for routine actions.
- Dashboards that impress in a screenshot but exhaust in daily use.
- Dark patterns, fake urgency, growth-hack nags.
- AI dressed up as magic. It's a fast, reliable colleague — not a wizard.
